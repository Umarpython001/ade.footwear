# Shop surfaces (Shop, Product, Cart, Checkout, Orders, shell)

Mode: Operate (Home hero is Persuade; built later section by section).
Audience: Instagram-referred Nigerian shoppers, phone and desktop equally.
Task: browse, pick a size, keep a guest cart, review a disabled checkout.
Constraints: AGENTS.md look is pinned; dummy catalogue marked [PLACEHOLDER].
Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The workshop bench at night. Shoes sit on dark wood under one warm lamp; the interface is the bench, not a luxury template. Refuses the generic black-and-gold boutique with glow, gradients and centred serif.

OWN-WORLD: Warm near-black ink (#0d0b09) and coal surfaces, bone-white type, one accent: the logo's ADE gold (#EDB52A) for primary actions, prices and focus only. Cognac and walnut leather tones as image backdrops and quiet fields. Bricolage Grotesque display, Schibsted Grotesk text. Pill buttons, 1px seam borders, photo-first cards with no card chrome.

STORY: Visitor sees real shoes immediately, understands price and sizes at a glance, adds a pair, and knows plainly that v1 takes order requests, not payment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shop: large display title left, category chips below, a two-column (mobile) / three-to-four column (desktop) grid of tall 4:5 photos with name and gold price under each. Product: photo dominant left, name/price/size grid/add-to-cart right; stacked on mobile with add-to-cart reachable.

FORM: Pinned by brief and designs/ mockups (dark full mockup); no concept roll. Seed key: none (brief-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
